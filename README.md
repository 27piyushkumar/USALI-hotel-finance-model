# USALI-Based Hotel Finance, Accounting & Revenue Model | 150-Room Luxury City Hotel

An end-to-end **Hotel Finance, Accounting & Revenue Management Model** developed in **Microsoft Excel** for a simulated 150-room luxury city hotel.

This portfolio project demonstrates how hotel operational data is transformed into complete financial reports through an integrated accounting workflow following the **Uniform System of Accounts for the Lodging Industry (USALI)**.

The model replicates real-world hotel finance processes, from revenue planning and departmental reporting to journal entries, financial statements, executive dashboards, and accounting controls.

---

# Project Overview

This project was designed to simulate the complete finance and accounting operations of a luxury hotel using industry-standard reporting practices.

It integrates operational planning, USALI departmental schedules, accounting records, financial statements, management reporting, and executive dashboards into a single Microsoft Excel workbook.
